\subsection{Discussion}
Once again, we saw two surprising nuances that were not clear from benchmark scores. First, we saw a
low correlation between problem difficulty and counterfeit understanding ability. Even for problems that
models pass often, we still found many samples they failed to understand. Second, we discovered that weaker models could generate counterfeits that were as difficult to understand
as stronger models. Overall, counterfeits were a relatively general phenomenon, not just a failure mode
of weaker models or harder problems.
